## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q1: Can mini portable air coolers run on a basic power bank or UPS?**  
*Yes, most USB-powered micro air coolers draw minimal voltage (5V), making them fully operational on standard portable power banks and home UPS systems.*

**Q2: How does adding mist/humidity help in cooling small rooms?**  
*Evaporative mist absorbs ambient heat from dry room air as it evaporates, dropping the local air temperature by several degrees around your seating space.*

**Q3: Are rechargeable fans durable enough for daily load-shedding hours in Pakistan?**  
*Yes, modern lithium-ion rechargeable mini fans provide consistent high-speed airflow for several hours on a single full charge.*
